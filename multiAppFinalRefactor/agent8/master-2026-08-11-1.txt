report saying there is nothing to lose.

Fix this first. It is live, it is data loss shaped, and it is the reason the
rest of the phase exists.

--- 2a. `packages/platform-email` — the prerequisite --------------------------
Everything else needs it. `apps/issues/lib/email/` is app-local because issues
was the only sender; open item 7 in `adding-an-app.md` says promote it rather
than copy it, and says what shape:

Sales then needs `RESEND_API_KEY` + `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL` (the human sets them).
**Write the code so a missing key degrades honestly**: `emailEnabled()` already

--- 2c. Password change signs out other sessions -------------------------------
Verify `password_changed_at` is bumped on the authenticated CHANGE path, not
only on RESET. Then prove it: two sessions, change the password in one, watch
the other stop working. **Observe it — do not read the code and conclude it.**

--- 2d. App-aware account deletion — the design ---------------------------------
This is the phase's centre. The user's specification:

  1. Deletion names every app the account is attached to, and what it holds
     there — not a count, the app names.
  2. The person chooses: **this app only**, or **all apps**.
  3. **"This app only" while other apps still hold data does NOT delete the
     account.** It deletes that app's data for that person and signs them out.
  4. "All apps" is the existing irreversible full deletion.

Three things that make this harder than it reads:

  * **No app can enumerate another app's data.** `sales_app` has no grant on
    `issues.*` — by design, and it is what CLAUDE.md finding #14 is about. So
    "which apps hold my data" cannot be answered by one deployment reading
    tables. Decide the mechanism and say why: a per-app callback, a
    `platform.apps`-driven fan-out over HTTP, or something better. **Whatever
    you pick must not be able to silently report "no data" for an app it simply
    could not reach** — that is the exact failure of the entity-drift reconciler.
  * **`DELETE /api/me` is one of only two capability exclusions in the parity
    test** (`CLAUDE.md`: "an agent must never delete its owner's account"). It
    stays human-only. If you add routes, they inherit that reasoning.
  * **Deleting a person's data is not the same as deleting a workspace.** A
    workspace with other members must survive. `deleteAccountReport`'s
    blocked/hard-delete split already encodes this — extend it, do not replace
    it.

4. WHAT NOT TO DO
================================================================================
- **Do not move `platform.comments`, `inbox_messages`, `workspaces` or any other
  now-issues-only table into `issues.*`.** The user asked; the answer is no, and
  the reason is the rule that made this whole refactor safe: no production row
  has been moved. Instead, if you have room, add a guard asserting no app but
  issues reads them — that turns a misleading NAME into an enforced BOUNDARY,
  which is the actual concern. Say so in `docs/platform-db.md`.
- Do not touch `platform.blob_references` or anything reaching `del()`.
- Do not weaken the whitelist. An ungated signup on ANY app is an ungated signup
  on every app — the account is shared.
- Do not deploy or release. The human does.
