5. `subject_urn`: KEPT, and the reasoning
================================================================================
Agent 2 asked; you left it to me. KEPT, and it turned out to be the phase's
cleanest line.

A URN is `bc:sales:<workspace-slug>/<type>/<number>`. Every part of that is in
`sales.*`: the slug is `sales.workspaces.slug`, the number is the row's `seq`.
`platform.entities` was where a URN was LOOKED UP; it was never what made one
true. So `resolveSubjectUrn` moved into `lib/db/queries/events.ts`, joins
`sales.workspaces` instead of `platform.workspaces`, and nothing else changed.

What that buys: `?subject_urn=` on the activity route and `bk activity --subject`
keep working against this app, and `bk sales prospect show` keeps printing an
address another app can resolve by hand. Dropping the column would have cost a
working read surface to save one nullable text field on a table that had no rows.

The integration suite asserts the derivation directly (`subject_urn` ==
`bc:sales:<slug>/prospect/<seq>` for a freshly created prospect), which is also
the check that would notice if it were ever filled from somewhere shared again.
