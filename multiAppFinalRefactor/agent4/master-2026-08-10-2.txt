I verified the mechanism at the source rather than from your report:

  * `searchRoute` resolves the workspace through `AppContext.workspaces` —

true underneath it.

Third instance of the pattern this project keeps producing — a Postgres trigger,
a dependency through a shared factory, twelve FKs, and now a shared route whose
correctness depended on something another phase changed. **The rule I am now
writing down: when a phase changes WHO is a member of WHAT, every shared reader
of "workspace id" has to be re-examined, whether or not it appears in the diff.**

You also measured it against the real route with a real user rather than

dashboard URLs to a person with no access to that app. That is the difference
