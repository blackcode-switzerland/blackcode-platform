3. ** WHERE THE PLAN IS WRONG. PHASE 2 AND PHASE 3 CANNOT SHIP SEPARATELY. **
================================================================================

This is the finding. Please read it before deciding anything else.

THE MECHANISM. Between this phase and Phase 3, `apps/sales` still writes four
PLATFORM tables that carry a `workspace_id` with a foreign key on
`platform.workspaces`:

    platform.events      (recordEvent, ~40 call sites)
    platform.entities    (the projection, every create)
    platform.labels      (label create)
    platform.uploads     (the ledger)

Those writes now carry a SALES workspace id. Two outcomes, and I measured both
rather than reasoning about them:

  (a) THE ID ALSO EXISTS IN platform.workspaces
      -> the FK is satisfied and the row lands against SOMEBODY ELSE'S
         WORKSPACE. Silent. Nothing reports it.

      Measured: I minted a sales workspace at id 2 and inserted a
      `platform.events` row for it. Accepted. `platform.workspaces` id 2 is
      "Bala's workspace", a different tenant.

  (b) THE ID DOES NOT EXIST THERE
      -> `insert or update on table "events" violates foreign key constraint
         events_workspace_id_workspaces_id_fk`. Loud, immediate, attributable.

WHY (a) IS THE LIKELY CASE AND NOT THE EDGE CASE: mirroring ids is what makes the
two spaces start out overlapping. `platform.workspaces` runs 1..13; the sales
sequence would have handed out 2, then 3, then 10 — straight into occupied
numbers. **The worst outcome was also the probable one.**

WHAT I DID ABOUT IT, and it is the whole of what I did:

  `0004` advances `sales.workspaces_id_seq` to
  `GREATEST(sales max, platform max, seq) + 1000`. Locally that is 1013, so the
  first self-signup got workspace 1014. Every mis-scoped write is now case (b).

  ** I converted silent cross-tenant contamination into a loud 500. I did NOT
     fix the 500. ** A brand-new sales workspace cannot create a prospect.

WHY I DID NOT PULL PHASE 3 FORWARD. Fixing it properly means pointing events,
entities and labels at `sales.*` — which is Phase 3's code half, ~40 call sites
plus the projection, and your plan says "one table at a time, deploy between
each, because doing them together makes a failure hard to attribute". Erasing
that boundary unilaterally, inside the phase whose diff is already the largest of
the eight, seemed exactly the wrong call. So the phase is complete as briefed and
this is yours.

THREE OPTIONS, with what I think:

  A. ** Deploy Phase 2 and Phase 3 together, or Phase 2 then Phase 3 with no
     users signing up in between. ** My recommendation. Phase 2 alone is
     deployable and SAFE — nothing corrupts, nothing is lost, existing sales
     data keeps working (§5 check 5) — it just cannot serve a NEW workspace
     until Phase 3 lands. Since nobody is signing up in the next few hours, the
     window costs nothing and the phase boundary survives for the record.

  B. Pull events + entities + labels forward into Phase 2. Makes this phase
     shippable standalone at the cost of the boundary and a much larger diff.

  C. Drop the four FKs on `platform.{events,entities,labels,uploads}`.
     ** DO NOT DO THIS, and I want the reason in writing so nobody re-litigates
     it in Phase 3: ** `deleteWorkspace` in `apps/issues` is a bare
     `DELETE FROM workspaces` and relies ENTIRELY on those cascades. Dropping
     them silently orphans an issues workspace's labels, events, entities and
     upload ledger rows on every workspace deletion, and verify.sh cannot see it
     (it fails on decreases, not on absent ones). It looks like the cheap fix
     and it is the expensive one.

WHAT PHASE 3'S AGENT NEEDS TO KNOW, whichever you pick:
