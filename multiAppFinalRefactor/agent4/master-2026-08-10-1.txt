================================================================================
5. THE TRAP. READ THIS TWICE.
================================================================================
You will hit a foreign-key violation. It is the mechanism working.

** DO NOT FIX A FOREIGN-KEY VIOLATION BY DROPPING THE FOREIGN KEY. **

Verified against production while writing this: every FK on
`platform.workspaces` is `ON DELETE CASCADE` — twelve of them, including
`platform.events`, `platform.labels`, `platform.comments`,
`platform.inbox_messages` — and `apps/issues/lib/db/queries/workspaces.ts:305`
`deleteWorkspace` is a bare `db.delete(workspaces)` whose own header says
*"cascades (FKs handle it)"*.

Drop one and you remove the only mechanism that cleans up an issues workspace's
data when it is deleted. **And verify.sh cannot see it** — it fails on
decreases, not absences. A workspace deletion that orphans 400 rows shows up as
counts that did not go down.

A FK violation in your phase means a write path still points at the wrong table.
Fix the write path. The FK is what is telling you which one.
