# b/billing — pitfalls

The mistakes that are specific to this app, and cost something real when made.
Each one reads as a success from the inside.

Related commands: `bk billing invoice void`, `bk billing invoice paid`,
`bk billing company edit`, `bk billing overview`, `bk billing history import`.

## A number is permanent, and a void is not a delete

An invoice number is contiguous with no holes and is never reused. Creating a
draft CONSUMES a number, so a draft created by mistake is voided, with a reason,
and the next bill takes the number after it. There is no delete, and no way to
hand a number back.

That makes a retried create expensive: if a create timed out and you run it
again, you may now have two real invoices. From another system, send an
`Idempotency-Key` with every create, so a retry replays instead of minting.

Keep the SAME key when you retry after a timeout — never mint a new one, because
the first request may have succeeded. If the retry answers
`idempotency_in_progress`, the first request is still running or was just
killed: wait and retry again with the same key. A claim whose request died is
released after a bounded wait, longer than any request may run, and the retry
then runs once.

## `vat_rate: null` is not `0`

Null means the line carries no VAT at all — an exempt act, or a company that is
not registered — and the bill shows no VAT block for it. Zero is a real rate: an
export or a reverse charge, and the bill shows a VAT line reading 0%. They are
different statements on the document and on a VAT return. Never write "no VAT"
as `--vat-rate 0`.

## EUR can never carry a QR reference

A QR reference needs a QR-IBAN and francs. An EUR bill uses a creditor reference
or none. The write door refuses the combination; do not work around it by
switching the currency after the fact — once a bill has left draft, its currency
and reference are frozen with the rest of the document.

## "Paid" is an assertion, not a reconciliation

Marking a bill paid records that a person or an agent says the money arrived, on
the date given. This app never looks at a bank account. Do not mark a bill paid
because it is due, or because a payment of roughly that size appeared somewhere;
matching payments to bills belongs to the books.

## The app schedules nothing

No bill is sent, reminded, or generated by this app on its own. If something
should happen on a date, an agent does it on that date.

## Money is per currency, and never merged

`bk billing overview` gives one line per currency and no grand total. Do not add
CHF to EUR to get "revenue": the result is not money in any currency. If you need
one figure, choose the currency.

## `--company` is where a wrong answer becomes a wrong IBAN

A workspace can bill from several companies, each with its own number sequence
and its own bank account. A list without `--company` interleaves them; an
invoice created for the wrong company is a legal document from the wrong entity,
payable into the wrong account. Say the company every time a workspace has more
than one.

A `--company` that names no company in this workspace is refused, never answered
with an empty list — so "no invoices" always means none, not "that slug does not
exist here".

## Editing a company does not reach a bill that already went out

An invoice takes its own copy of the issuing company the moment it leaves draft,
and renders from that copy forever: its account, its creditor name and its totals
do not move when the company is edited. So a corrected IBAN fixes the drafts and
every future bill, and **not** the unpaid ones already with clients — those still
name the old account, exactly as the copy the client holds does. If that account
is wrong, void each one with a reason and reissue it.

## A dash your keyboard typed can stop a bill

A Swiss QR Code carries Latin letters, accents and plain punctuation. An em dash,
a curly quote or an emoji in the payment message is refused when you write it,
naming the character and where it is. Nothing is replaced for you; retype it.

## The archive is not a list of debts

Imported history is a record of what the old systems issued. An unpaid archived
bill is not in the overview and nothing here chases it. Import is all or nothing,
a repeated row is refused rather than skipped, and every amount is a string —
`bk guide billing/imported-history` has the rest.

## Sending needs the document, and the document is not served yet

`bk billing invoice send` refuses before doing anything until the PDF renderer
is wired in. A bill delivered another way — paper, another mailbox — is recorded
with `bk billing invoice mark-sent`, which leaves no message id: that absence is
how the record says this app did not send it.
