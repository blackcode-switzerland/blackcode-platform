1. THE SCAFFOLD
================================================================================
`apps/_scaffold/lib/api.ts` currently says its workspaces come from
`platform.workspaces` "for now", and names this phase as where that changes. It
was right.

An app copied from the scaffold today would be born with the coupling this
refactor just spent six phases removing. Give it:

  * its own `scaffold.workspaces` / `workspace_members` / `invitations`
  * self-signup **with the whitelist gate** — see §2
  * a workspace on first sign-in, in one transaction with its membership
  * a members page, visible by default
  * no `requireAppAccess`, no `workspace_apps`, no `app_access` — they are gone
  * `AppContext.workspaces` supplying its OWN source, required not optional

**Keep the prefixed TypeScript names** (`scaffoldWorkspaces` →
`scaffold.workspaces`). `schema.ts` re-exports the platform schema, so a bare
`export const workspaces` shadows `platform.workspaces` silently at every import
site. Agent 2 found that; agent 3 and 4 both relied on it.

The scaffold is never deployed. It must build, lint, typecheck and pass every
guardrail, and it must not appear in `devops/release.sh`'s registry.

================================================================================
