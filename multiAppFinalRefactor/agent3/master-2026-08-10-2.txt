--- 1a. I VERIFIED OPTION C IS FORBIDDEN, AND IT IS WORSE THAN YOU WROTE -------
You asked for the reason in writing so nobody re-litigates it in Phase 3. Here
it is, checked against production rather than repeated from your report:

  * EVERY foreign key on `platform.workspaces` is `ON DELETE CASCADE` — twelve
    of them, including `platform.events`, `platform.labels`,
    `platform.comments`, `platform.inbox_messages` and five `issues.*` tables.
  * `apps/issues/lib/db/queries/workspaces.ts:305` `deleteWorkspace` is a bare
    `db.delete(workspaces)`, and its own header at line 9 says
    *"deleteWorkspace cascades (FKs handle it)"*.

So dropping those FKs does not merely orphan rows — it removes the ONLY
mechanism by which deleting an issues workspace cleans up its own labels,
events, comments and inbox. And you named the reason it would never be caught:
**verify.sh fails on decreases, not on absences.** A workspace deletion that
leaves 400 orphaned rows behind shows up as counts that did NOT go down.

That is a genuinely new gap in the instrument, found by reasoning about a
proposal rather than by running anything. It goes in agent 4's brief.

**Required, not optional, is the detail I would have got wrong.** An optional

to remember, and the failure silent: a new app that forgot it serves correctly
against another app's tenancy. `_scaffold` failing typecheck the moment the
field appeared is that decision working on its first day.

The two `apps/issues` changes are both justified and both correctly flagged
rather than buried. `storage_limit_bytes` in particular: the alternative was
putting a field only one app has ever had into the shared interface, which is
what the header forbids.
