================================================================================
1. THE PROBLEM, IN THE USER'S WORDS
================================================================================
Looking at sales' Settings → Account, which sends people to issues for a
password change and to issues to close their account:

  > "sales is independent, but the user login is shared. That means a user
  >  created in sales or issues can reset their password in sales or issues,
  >  right? … Where in sales I have to go to issues app."

And at sales' login page, which says *"Access is by invitation"*:

  > "as sales is independent, through sales can also a user account be created
  >  right? also supporting forget password screens etc."

**Both are true and neither is a policy decision.** Sales cannot send email, so
it cannot deliver a one-time code, so it defers to issues. Everything on that
screen traces to `apps/issues/lib/email/` never having become a package.


================================================================================
2. WHAT ALREADY EXISTS — DO NOT REBUILD
================================================================================
I checked, so you do not spend the phase discovering it.

  * `POST /api/auth/register` exists on **both** apps, with the whitelist gate.
  * Issues has the full reset API already:
        /api/auth/password-reset/request  · /confirm
        /api/me/password/request-otp      · /confirm
  * `packages/platform-auth/src/password-reset.ts` is where the logic lives.
  * **`users.password_changed_at` already invalidates every other session**,
    verified end to end by agent 8 against two real sessions.

**So the missing pieces are: the email PACKAGE, sales' half of the reset API,
and the SCREENS. Neither app has a signup screen or a forgot-password screen.**
The capability has existed for months with no front door.


================================================================================
3. WHAT TO BUILD
================================================================================

--- 3a. `packages/platform-email` ---------------------------------------------
`adding-an-app.md` open item 7 says promote, do not copy, and names the shape:

  * the ADDRESS is platform-wide — `admin@blackcode.ch`, the apex domain,
    because Resend's free plan verifies ONE domain per account
  * the APP IDENTITY is the display name: `Blackcode Issues <admin@…>`
  * so `fromAddress()` takes the app, the same un-hardcoding as `uploads.app`

**Agent 8 says this is larger than "promote a module" (§5.3) and every estimate
on this project that assumed a move was mechanical has been wrong.** If it is
larger than one phase with §3b, say so and stop — that judgement has been right
every time an agent made it.

`emailEnabled()` already exists. **An app without a key must degrade honestly**,
not silently not-send. A reset that reports success and delivers nothing is
worse than one that refuses.

--- 3b. The front doors, in BOTH apps ------------------------------------------
  * `/signup` — posting to the register route that already exists. **The
    whitelist gate must hold, and you must WATCH a non-whitelisted email be
    refused**, in the browser, not in a unit test. The account is shared: an
    ungated signup on any app is an ungated signup on every app.
  * `/forgot-password` and the code-entry step. Sales needs the API half too.
  * Sales' Settings → Account stops deferring to issues.

**Sales' login page says "Access is by invitation. Ask a workspace owner to
invite you and grant you b/sales."** The second half is already false — the
grant was `app_access`, dropped in Phase 5. Fix the whole sentence in the same
commit as the signup screen. Agent 7 found the landing page selling `bk undo`
for months; a page describing a capability the product does not have, or now
does, is the same defect.

Each app's own visual language. Sales' colours are decided in
`apps/sales/lib/pipeline.ts` — agent 2 was caught by a guard for importing
issues' grey.

4. WHAT NOT TO DO
================================================================================
- **Do not weaken the whitelist.** It is the only thing standing between a
  public signup form and a shared account on every app.
- Do not send real email to anyone but yourself in testing.
- Do not touch `platform.blob_references` or anything reaching `del()`.
- Do not move `platform.*` tables into `issues.*` — declined twice.
- Do not deploy or set production env vars. The human does both.
