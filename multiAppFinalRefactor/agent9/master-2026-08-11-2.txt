1. §2.3 — THE FALSE PREMISE. THIS IS THE FINDING.
================================================================================
Agent 8 wrote, and I quoted into your brief as settled:

  > "a wrong or stale `base_url` … would make an app look unreachable, which now
  >  BLOCKS deletion — a safe direction, worth stating."

**It is safe only for an address that answers nothing.** An address pointing at
another app in the suite answers confidently, as that app. You measured it:

    Blackcode Issues   reachable=True   [{'label':'issues','count':2}]
    Sales              reachable=True   [{'label':'issues','count':2}]

Two apps, one set of data, reported twice under two names, no warning anywhere.
And then `scope=all_apps` purges the issues origin twice, reads issues' own
"I am empty" as sales' answer, **asserts the census satisfied**, and closes the
account over an untouched sales workspace.

**That is this phase's own bug, reached through a config error, past every guard
written for it** — including the reachable/unreachable type, which was the
safety property the whole design rested on. The type made "no answer" and "no
data" distinguishable. It could not make "the wrong app's answer" and "this
app's answer" distinguishable, because both are answers.

I verified the fix is in: `account-census.ts` rejects a reply whose `app` is not
the app addressed, on the census AND on the purge — and your note that it
matters more on the purge is the sharper half: *by the time a purge answers, it
has already deleted something.*

**How you found it is the part I want on the record.** Not review, not a test —
the standing rule's second question, *what would this still pass on?*, asked
against two running dev servers. It was green. Every guard in the phase was
green. This is the ninth phase in a row where that question found something
nothing else could, and the first where what it found was in the design rather
than in an implementation.

And the fix was free because every app already named itself in its reply. **The
information needed to catch it was already on the wire and nobody had thought to
read it** — which is the same shape as `error_events.workspace_id` and
`transaction_log`: a value present, carried, and unexamined.
